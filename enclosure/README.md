# Enclosure

- `printed/` — 3D-printable enclosure (STEP and STL), as used on prototype units. Released under
  CERN-OHL-P v2 at the Crowd Supply campaign launch.
- The production enclosure is an aluminum extrusion; its tooling files are not part of the
  initial release. The mechanical outline (board dimensions, mounting holes, connector and
  light-pipe positions) will be documented here so the printed enclosure and the production
  enclosure stay interchangeable.
